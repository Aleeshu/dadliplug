# Operations

Manual, non-code processes the team runs. Not yet written up.

## Onboarding

A business is onboarded by hand. Flow: business submits info → team implements
the listing → team verifies → business gets admin access → business can edit.

Checklist to write in `onboarding/`. Photos will arrive as bad phone snaps;
budget cleanup time.

## Sales

Recruit the first 5–10 businesses before launch, then go industry by industry.
Target is businesses currently doing their own ads badly.

## Moderation

Reviews are live-then-moderated. Users report, team reviews and removes.
Businesses can dispute a review or opt out of reviews entirely.

Team admin also handles: listing creation and edits, subscription status when a
transfer lands, and delisting non-payers.
