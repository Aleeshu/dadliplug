# Pricing test and decision rules (Phase 9)

Decide these with the twenty interviews in front of you, not before. The
numbers come from **Team → Listings → Leads → Answers** and from how many
trials convert. The thresholds below are set now, before the answers arrive,
so the answers cannot quietly move them.

## What is being tested

| Question | Evidence | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Will owners pay at all? | Trials started, and trials that pay after 3 days | Fewer than 3 paying of the first 10 trials: stop and rethink the offer before launch. |
| Is Basic (EC$50) the right default? | Share leaning to Basic or above | Under 40%: make Growing (EC$25) the default pitch and lead with it. |
| Is Featured (EC$100) worth twice Basic? | Share leaning to Featured; what they said would make it worth it | Under 10%: Featured needs more weight (more Spotlight weeks, ads included) or a lower price. Do not lower it on fewer than 20 answers. |
| Team-maintained or self-edit? (plan.md 15, decision 1) | "Who keeps the listing" answers | Team over 60%: keep the team model and say it loudly in the pitch. Self over 60%: keep the editor front and centre, and price the team's upkeep as a Basic feature. Between: offer both and watch which they use. |
| Where do they advertise now? | Channels answer | If most say Facebook or WhatsApp status, the pitch is "your Facebook post disappears in a day; your listing does not". |
| What do they spend now? | Spend band | If most spend nothing, EC$25 is a new expense, not a switch. Lead with the free trial and the monthly report. |
| Do customers come? | Views and taps in the first trials' monthly reports | A first report under 30 views does not renew anyone. Do not launch to businesses until there is a customer audience plan. |

## How to read small numbers

Twenty answers is enough to spot a strong preference and not enough to
measure a weak one. Treat anything within 10 points of a threshold as
"unclear", and ask five more owners in that segment before deciding.

## What not to do

- Do not drop the price because one owner said it was too much.
- Do not count "sounds good" as a yes. Only a started trial counts.
- Do not change two things at once. If you change the price, keep the pitch.

## The essentials-layer question (plan.md 15)

Also ask a few customers, not only owners: "Would you open the app for
pharmacies, fuel stations and ATMs, even when you are not looking for a
business?" If most say yes, and the paying owners in the interviews do not
object to free essentials appearing next to them, add a small, clearly
labelled essentials set. If owners object, do not.

## Record the outcome

Write each decision into plan.md section 15, with the count behind it and the
date, and close Phase 9.
