# Owner interviews (Phase 9)

Twenty conversations with business owners, before launch. The point is to learn,
not to sell: plan.md section 16 lists the risks these answer, and none of them
is technical.

## Who

Aim for a spread, not twenty restaurants:

| Kind | How many |
| --- | --- |
| Food and drink | 5 |
| Salons, barbers, beauty | 3 |
| Trades and car work | 3 |
| Shops | 3 |
| Tours and entertainment | 2 |
| Online-only sellers | 2 |
| Real estate, education, other | 2 |

Mix St. John's with English Harbour, Jolly Harbour and the villages. Include at
least five businesses that **do not** advertise at all today.

## Record every one in the app

Use the app's **Get your business listed** form (More menu) for each
conversation, filled in on the owner's behalf, then add a team note in
**Team → Listings → Leads**. The Answers tab counts everything, so nobody keeps
a spreadsheet and the totals are never stale. Set the source to "interview" in
the note if it was not the owner typing.

## The conversation (15 minutes)

Ask open questions first. Do not mention prices until section 3.

### 1. How customers find them now

- How did your last three new customers find you?
- Where do you advertise today? What does it cost you a month?
- What has worked? What was a waste of money?
- How do customers ask about hours or prices? (Phone, WhatsApp, Facebook
  messages, walk-ins?)

### 2. Keeping information right

- When your hours change, or you close for a day, how do people find out?
- Has a customer turned up when you were closed because something online was
  wrong?
- If we kept your listing up to date for you, would you message us changes on
  WhatsApp? Or would you rather edit it yourself?

That last question is **decision 1 in plan.md section 15**. Record the answer
as "team", "self" or "unsure".

### 3. Price (show the app first)

Show them their category on the map, a listing, the Featured page and a
monthly report. Then:

- Growing is EC$25 a month, Basic EC$50, Featured EC$100. Which would you
  pick, if any?
- What would make Featured worth EC$100 to you?
- At what price would Basic be too expensive to consider? At what price would
  it seem too cheap to be any good?

Record the plan they lean to. Do not argue with the answer.

### 4. Commitment (the only answer that really counts)

- Would you like to try it free for 3 days, starting now?

A yes here is worth ten "sounds good"s. If yes, set the lead to **On trial**,
build the listing (or import it, see `scripts/import_listings.py`), verify it
in person, and start the trial in Team → Billing.

## After each one

- Fill in the lead and the team note the same day.
- Note anything they asked for that the app does not do. Three requests for the
  same thing is a signal; one is an anecdote.
- Thank them. Small island: they will talk to each other.

## When twenty are done

Read `pricing-test.md` and make the calls it lists. Write the decisions into
plan.md section 15 with the numbers that support them.
