# Delete your Dadliplug account

## In the app

1. Open Dadliplug and sign in.
2. Go to More, then Your account.
3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE, and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

## Without the app

If you no longer have the app, contact us from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm when it is done, within 30 days at most.

## What is deleted

Your account and email address, your reviews, check-ins, saved places, follows, saved events, the people you hid, any listing claims, and your notification tokens.

## What is kept

- Counts of listing views, calls and taps, as totals with no link to you.
- Reports you sent about reviews or listings, without your name, so moderation decisions still make sense.
- A business you managed is not deleted. It stays with the Dadliplug team.
