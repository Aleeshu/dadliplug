# Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Dadliplug is a map and directory of businesses in Antigua and Barbuda. This policy says what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. We collect as little as the app needs to work.

## What we collect

### If you only browse

You can use the map, search, listings, offers and events without an account. We count listing views, calls and WhatsApp taps so businesses can see how they are doing. When you are not signed in, these counts are not linked to you.

### Your location

When you use "Near me" or the map's location button, your phone's location is used on your phone to sort and centre results. It is never sent to us or stored.

### If you create an account

- Your email address, and the name you give, or the ones Google shares if you sign in with Google.
- Reviews you write, and the check-ins (QR scans at a business) that let you write them.
- Places you save, businesses you follow and events you save.
- Who you have hidden, so their reviews stay hidden from you.
- Reports you send about a review or a listing.
- If you claim a business: the name, role and phone number you give, so the team can check the claim.
- A notification token from your phone, if you allow notifications.
- When you view, call or message a business while signed in, we record it once per hour per business, to stop the same person being counted many times.

## Why we use it

- To run your account and show your reviews, saved places and follows.
- To check that reviews come from real visits.
- To send the notifications you asked for: announcements from businesses you follow and review requests after a visit.
- To rank businesses fairly. Rank comes from ratings, review count and customer activity. Nobody can pay for a higher rank.
- To act on reports and keep the app safe.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising profiles. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

## Who else handles it

- **Supabase** hosts our database and accounts.
- **Google** provides sign-in (if you choose it), the map (Google Maps), and push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging). Google's own privacy policy applies to those services.
- **Cloudflare** serves the public web page for each listing.

These providers may store data outside Antigua and Barbuda. They process it only to provide their service to us.

## What others can see

Your reviews are public, shown with your display name. Businesses can see their own view, call and tap counts as totals, not who made them. A business sees the name on a review request you send them.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Activity counts are kept as totals for businesses. Reports are kept after an account is deleted, without your name, so moderation decisions still make sense.

## Your choices and rights

- **Delete your account** at any time: Profile, then Delete account. This removes your account, reviews, check-ins, saved places, follows, hidden-people list, claims and notification tokens. It cannot be undone.
- **Turn off notifications** in your phone's settings.
- **Ask for a copy** of your data, or ask us to correct it, by contacting us below.

## Children

Dadliplug is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change applies.
